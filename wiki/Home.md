## About the Project

RetourMarche allows users to:
- Browse and search a catalogue of refurbished **smartphones** and **accessories**
- Manage a **shopping cart** and place orders
- Use **promo codes** and **product insurance** options
- Authenticate via a secure login / registration system

Administrators can manage inventory, users, and orders through a dedicated **dashboard**.
